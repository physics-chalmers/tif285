Hi everyone,

Welcome to the course "Learning from data".  We start on Monday August 31st at 13.15 in the HA3 lecture hall, with the first lecture and introduction to the course. 

The course aims to teach a physicist's approach to Learning from data. It is project-based with python and Jupyter Notebooks as the computational environment. The examination is based on problem sets and projects. There will be face-to-face discussions with the teaching assistants on your problem set solutions plus an oral exam at the end of the course, focusing on the learning outcomes from your solutions to the projects.

Getting ready for the course you are welcome to follow the getting started guide on the course web page. There is a dedicated getting-started session on the first Monday afternoon where the teachers will demonstrate the download of course material and setup of the python environment. At the end of this demonstration you will have a chance to ask questions and to get help with your own installation. Note that this is the preferred opportunity for getting-started assistance. The other lab sessions will be focused on exercises, problem sets and projects.

The course is accompanied by an online book that includes lecture notes, interactive demonstrations and exercise notebooks. This resource is published open-access (https://nucleartalent.github.io/LFD_for_Physicists) in the Jupyter Book format. You are in particular encouraged to get prepared by studying the text and guides in Appendix C for the use of Python, Jupyter Notebooks and NumPy. You are also encouraged to review Appendix A that contains important concepts in mathematical statistics. 

The exercise notebooks can be downloaded  in the ipynb format from the Jupyter Book and can then be run in your newly installed python environment. 

All lectures and the computer lab sessions will be on campus. Note in particular that the Monday lab session typically starts with a demonstration or feedback discussion led by one of the teaching assistants. 

Throughout the course, we will be using the Yata Discussions forum to facilitate recurrent group discussions between students and with teachers. See the Online teaching and extra resources.
